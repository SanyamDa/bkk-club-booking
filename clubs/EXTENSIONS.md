This is the pipeline of possible extensions to the platform: loyalty, targeted offers, birthday specials, review requests and everything else that becomes possible once a club's bookings, chats, guests and spend run through us. It exists so the ideas are kept in one place and can be picked from when there is demand.

**How it was made.** Five research passes, each from a different angle (what competing products sell, what LINE itself allows in Thailand, the guest's journey from discovery to repeat visit, the club's back office and our own revenue, and what AI adds), produced 226 raw ideas. They were merged into the 170 items below. The platform and legal claims the items depend on were then checked against the source documents. **The checks are research, not legal advice**, and items marked unverified in the text have not been confirmed.

**Owner decisions that apply to every item.** We are the data controller, and guests are told at first contact (SPEC.md §5.5, §13). Privacy-law notes in the constraint column are research notes, not preconditions. LINE's and Meta's terms (SPEC.md §14.8) and alcohol law are real constraints.

## How to read it

- **Effort:** S = days, M = weeks, L = months. Rough.
- **Horizon:** **Next** = cheap once bookings and guest data exist. **Later** = needs POS spend data or several clubs. **Far** = needs scale, partners or licences.
- **Needs** says what must exist first. Many items need nothing beyond what SPEC.md already plans.
- **Main constraint** is the thing most likely to stop or reshape the item.

## What shapes this backlog

Seven findings from the checks decide which ideas are worth building and in what form. Each affects many items.

1. **Rewards must be access and service, not cheaper alcohol.** Thai alcohol law (the Alcoholic Beverage Control Act, amended in force from 8 Nov 2025) restricts price cuts, giveaways and benefits tied to buying alcohol, and restricts alcohol advertising. Loyalty therefore works best as **status and treatment**: priority booking, better tables, deposit waived, queue skip, a host's greeting. Points earned on drinks and spent on drinks carry the highest risk. Discounted or free drinks as a reward are in the dropped list.
2. **Birthdays have to come from our own form.** LINE gives birth date and phone number only to approved corporate accounts in Japan. Thai accounts get the name and picture. Birthday and occasion offers depend on the guest telling us in the booking form.
3. **Paid ads are closed for this business.** Meta's alcohol ad policy lists Thailand among countries where alcohol ads cannot run at all, and counts venues whose business depends on alcohol sales. LINE's Thai ad policy also prohibits alcohol. Growth comes through the club's own LINE messages, referrals and links, not ad retargeting.
4. **Every targeted message costs money.** LINE counts push and targeted messages per recipient against the club's plan. Campaign features need a cost preview and a frequency cap built in.
5. **Prize draws need a permit.** A chance-based prize tied to a business needs a licence under the Gambling Act, and commentary says the winner must be drawn physically in public. LINE's lottery coupons do not remove that requirement.
6. **Review requests go to everyone, with no reward.** Asking only happy guests, or offering something for a review, breaks the review platforms' rules. The compliant form is one neutral request to every guest after a visit, with a private feedback route beside it.
7. **WhatsApp cannot carry alcohol promotion in Thailand.** Bookings and reminders are fine. Offers are not.

Two more that affect scope: a **verified LINE account** (about THB 888, one-time) unlocks several LINE features other items rely on; and LINE's **coupon API** (released Aug 2025) can create coupons but does not report who redeemed them.
